Philosophy

Systems that hold up after the demo ends

I started out maintaining desktops and networks before moving into full-stack development in 2019 at Cokreates, working on ERP and e-Government platforms. That early exposure to government-scale systems shaped how I still work — favor clean, reusable architecture over quick wins, because these systems outlive the sprint they were built in.

"A reusable Admin or Security module built once saves every team that touches the system after you."

At Doer Services I led core architecture and reusable modules for enterprise frameworks; at Celloscope I owned reporting pipelines and production troubleshooting. Across every role, the throughline is the same: gather the real requirement, design for the edge case, and leave the codebase easier to extend than I found it.
